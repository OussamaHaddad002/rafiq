# Patient policies - Clinique Rafiq

## Cancellations and missed appointments

- Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment, by phone (+216 70 000 100) or WhatsApp (+216 20 000 100).
- A late cancellation (less than 24 hours before) or a missed appointment without notice is charged a fee of 20 DT, payable at your next visit.
- The fee is waived for documented emergencies.

## Late arrival

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, the doctor may not be able to see you and your appointment may be moved to the next available slot.

## Minors

Patients under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian, who signs the consent form at reception.

## Payment

- Payment is made at reception before the consultation.
- We accept cash and bank cards (Visa and Mastercard). Cheques are not accepted.
- An itemized invoice is provided for every payment.

## What to bring

- A valid ID card or passport
- Your insurance card, if you have one
- Your prescription or referral letter, if you have one
- Previous test results and imaging (X-rays, scans)

## Privacy and medical records

- Your medical data is kept confidential and is never shared with third parties without your written consent, except with your insurer when you request reimbursement.
- Medical records are kept for 20 years.
- You can request a copy of your medical record in writing at reception. It is provided within 7 working days, free of charge.

## Complaints

You can send a complaint by email to contact@clinique-rafiq.example or fill in the form at reception. We reply within 10 working days.
